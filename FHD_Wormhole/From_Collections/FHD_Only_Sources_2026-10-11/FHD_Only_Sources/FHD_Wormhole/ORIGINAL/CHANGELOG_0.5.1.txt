FHD_Wormhole 0.5.1 Alpha — equipment removal crash hotfix

- Fixed crash when removing a Wormhole-capable engine.
- Root cause: RScript does not reliably short-circuit && / || expressions.
  A condition such as if(engine && ItemExtraSpecialsCountByType(engine,...))
  could still call ItemExtraSpecialsCountByType with engine == 0.
- Replaced pointer-dependent compound guards with explicit nested checks.
- Applied the same protection to hull handling, legacy migration, the ship
  button check, durability processing, and empty ShipItems slots.
- Balance, shop scanning, AutoSearch, cooldown and generation rules are unchanged.
